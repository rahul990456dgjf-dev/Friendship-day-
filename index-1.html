Chapter One

Our Friendship Story

It all started in the middle of a game — Free Fire. Two strangers, each on their own adventure, crossed paths in a world built for battle. But what began as a casual encounter slowly grew into something far more meaningful than either of us expected.

After that first meeting, we started talking. Not just about the game, but about everything. Hours would pass and it still felt like there was so much left to say. Time had a strange way of disappearing whenever we were in conversation.

Slowly, we began to understand each other — our humour, our moods, the little things that made us laugh and the bigger things that mattered to us. Every conversation peeled back another layer, and every layer made the friendship feel more real, more valuable.

Even the most ordinary days stopped feeling ordinary. A boring afternoon could turn into something memorable simply because there was someone on the other end of the screen ready to talk, ready to listen, ready to laugh.

It became comfortable — the kind of comfort you rarely find. The kind where you don't have to pretend or perform. You could just be yourself, share your honest thoughts, and know they'd be received with warmth.

And that is how our friendship became one of the happiest parts of life — quietly, beautifully, and without even trying. 🌸
